Reseller Program FAQ

Commissions are paid monthly, around the 15th of each month for the previous month's sales. Annual prepayment bonuses are paid within 30 days of the customer's payment.

There's no minimum sales requirement to join the program. However, to maintain Silver tier status, you need to maintain at least TZS 5M in monthly sales, and for Gold tier, TZS 15M in monthly sales.

We provide comprehensive support including product training, sales materials, demo accounts, marketing collateral, and dedicated account managers for Silver and Gold tier resellers. We also offer co-marketing funds for qualified partners.

Yes, you can resell across Tanzania. We offer territory protection for established resellers to ensure you have exclusive rights to your designated regions as you grow your business.
